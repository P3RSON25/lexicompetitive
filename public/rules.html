HOW TO PLAY

01 / GOAL

Be the last board standing.

Rooms are PVP and need two players to start.

02 / GRAMS

You hold three two-letter grams.

Submit a dictionary word containing at least one gram. Accepted words deal damage and give you three new grams.

03 / ATTACK

  • 1 gram = 1 line.
  • 2 grams = 3 lines plus combo.
  • 3 grams = 6 lines plus combo, sent to every living opponent.
  • A word made entirely by chaining your grams has its attack halved, rounded down.

04 / COMBO

Two-gram words build combo up to 4.

One-gram words reset combo. Three-gram words cash it out, then reset it.

05 / GARBAGE

Incoming lines wait in pending for 4 seconds.

The timer can extend for 8 incoming attacks. Later attacks still add lines without extending the timer.

20 locked lines eliminates a player.

06 / DEFENSE

Pending garbage is canceled first.

With no pending garbage, your attack clears locked lines while still being sent in full.

07 / TARGET

  • KO targets the opponent with the most locked lines.
  • EQUAL targets the opponent with the fewest locked lines.
  • RANDOM targets any living opponent.
  • Three-gram attacks always hit every living opponent.

08 / ROUND

When the round ends, your WPM is shown.

The room stays open. The host can start another round.
